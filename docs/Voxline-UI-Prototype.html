Will callers know they are talking to an AI?

Most do not ask. The agent introduces itself by your agency name and speaks naturally. If a caller asks directly, it answers honestly and offers to put them through to a human on your escalation number.

Do we have to change our phone number?

No. You forward your existing line to Voxline, either always or only after hours and on busy. You can revert the forwarding at any moment without involving us.

What happens when we run out of included minutes?

The agent keeps answering. Extra minutes are billed at your plan's per-minute rate on the next invoice, and the portal shows your usage against the plan every day so it is never a surprise.

Can it book trips or take payments?

Not in v1, by design. Voxline qualifies and captures. Your consultants sell. Anything involving money or a confirmed booking is handed to a human.

How long does onboarding take?

Five business days. Day one we script your agent from a 45-minute call, days two to four we test it against real scenarios with you, day five you go live.
